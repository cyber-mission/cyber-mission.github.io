# Our Mission

To develop the foundational multidisciplinary tools needed to de-risk environmentally focused engineering biology and ultimately support its future deployment into real-world ecosystems.


Engineering biology offers new ways to tackle environmental challenges such as pollution and the removal of harmful compounds. Yet before engineered organisms can be released outside the laboratory, we need to understand how they behave in complex, living ecosystems, and how to be confident they are safe.

The CYBER Mission Award brings together researchers from across disciplines, including biology, engineering, data science, metrology and the social sciences, to build the shared tools, data and standards this requires. Our work centres on cyanobacteria, photosynthetic microbes that play a vital role in natural environments and that can be reprogrammed to perform useful functions.

Our programme combines:

- **Ecological "wind tunnels"** that mimic real-world complexity, so engineered organisms can be tested under realistic conditions before any deployment.
- **High-throughput experiments and AI** to build genetic toolkits and learn from diverse data sets, making cellular reprogramming faster and more reliable.
- **Traceability and cyber-bio security**, so engineered cells can be tracked in natural environments and trusted.
- **Standards and metrology** that bring measurement rigour to biological engineering.
- **Community building**, so the people and teams needed to realise the long-term benefits of this research are connected.
