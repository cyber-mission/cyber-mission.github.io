Daniel is part of the National Measurement Laboratory (NML) team supporting CYBER. He will be working on the application of digital PCR methods and standards to measure genetic material across the project, helping to provide accurate and reliable measurements of the DNA and RNA underpinning the engineered cells and environmental samples studied by CYBER.
